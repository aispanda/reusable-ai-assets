# Research brief — AI-Age Engineering Philosophy

Act as an independent engineering-research lead. Produce a source-backed, business-readable synthesis that can strengthen a vendor-neutral AI-Age Engineering philosophy. The current operating frame is **The 9 Sutras of AI-Age Software Engineering** plus the **99/10** efficiency shorthand in `ENGINEERING_PRINCIPLES.template.md`. Test every hypothesis; do not endorse slogans because they appear in this prompt.

## Hypotheses to test

1. AI shifts the bottleneck from writing code to defining intent, architecture, evaluation, security and governance (**Sutra 1**).
2. “99/10” is useful shorthand for near-complete practical quality at radically lower cost, not a ratio that deserves separate calculation effort (**Sutra 9** guardrail).
3. Standard repository-owned code and interchangeable model adapters outperform proprietary lock-in when AI reduces implementation effort; total lifecycle cost and exit risk remain decisive (**Sutra 3**).
4. Deterministic fitness functions and human approval gates remain necessary even when AI performs most implementation (**Sutras 2 and 8**).
5. Decoupling ingestion from model processing and scale-to-zero hosting improve economics without sacrificing readiness (**Sutras 4 and 7**).
6. Pilot benchmarking on real business data plus role-based multi-agent routing beats public leaderboards for production model choice (**Sutra 5**).
7. Versioned prompts, context pipelines, evals and reusable assets create compounding organizational capability (**Sutra 6**).
8. Open-source and open-standard first improves adaptability and economics when maturity, maintenance, security and licensing are verified.

## Research domains

- software-engineering classics, modularity, simplicity and technical debt;
- Lean, Agile, DevOps, SRE, continuous delivery and empirical productivity;
- evolutionary architecture, option value, build-versus-buy and total-cost economics;
- open-source governance, licensing, maintainership and software-supply-chain security;
- human-AI software development, agentic coding, context engineering, evals and model routing;
- low-code/no-code economics in an AI-generated-code environment;
- organizational learning, reusable knowledge, double-loop learning, Kaizen and learning organizations;
- safety, privacy, accountability, accessibility and sustainable operations.

Use influential books, peer-reviewed papers, standards, respected empirical studies and current primary documentation. For books, cite author, title, edition/year and relevant chapter/theme; use lawful summaries/previews and avoid long quotations. Identify funding or vendor bias. Prefer original sources over commentary.

Start from the seed set and current mappings in [`AI_AGE_ENGINEERING_EVIDENCE_MATRIX.md`](AI_AGE_ENGINEERING_EVIDENCE_MATRIX.md). Challenge weak or missing support—especially Sutras 7–9 commercial/ops claims—rather than rubber-stamping the matrix.

## Required output

Keep the complete response under 2,500 words.

1. **Executive synthesis** — maximum 250 words.
2. **Evidence matrix** — maximum 20 highest-value sources: source, key finding, support/challenge, applicability and limitation.
3. **Recommended principles** — maximum 12; each has plain-language rule, evidence, boundary/counterexample and measurable test.
4. **Efficiency guardrails** — explain what can be removed or automated and what quality, safety, evidence and verification must never be sacrificed. Do not calculate a 99/10 ratio.
5. **Contradictions and failure modes** — where the philosophy could become unsafe, uneconomic or dogmatic.
6. **Proposed edits** — concise additions/amendments/deletions; do not edit files.
7. **Research gaps** — only questions requiring further evidence or human decision.

Label material claims `OBSERVED` or `INFERRED`, cite direct links near claims, and distinguish evidence from recommendation. Keep final principles vendor-neutral; organizations may appear only in source attribution. Do not use promotional language or invent numerical support for the 99/10 target.
