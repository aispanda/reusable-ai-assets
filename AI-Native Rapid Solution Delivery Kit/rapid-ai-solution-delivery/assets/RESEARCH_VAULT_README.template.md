# Research Vault

Store project-specific standards and implementation evidence here; it is not project commitment. Named comparative or inspiration research belongs in a separately designated private evidence corpus outside product, reusable, and public roots.

## Structure

- `sources/`: saved or linked implementation standards and provider material required by the project.
- `notes/`: concise implementation findings, dates, limitations, and affected decision IDs.
- External private evidence corpus: named comparative observations, prices, licence facts, and source links only; no consuming-product identifiers or decisions.

## Rules

1. Prefer primary/current sources; record access date and direct link only in the applicable evidence corpus.
2. Enforce the two-corpus boundary: named comparative evidence has no consumer identifiers, paths, architecture, requirements, priorities, backlog or decisions; product and reusable roots have no named comparative details, claims or citations.
3. Separate sourced fact, analysis, and inference. Respect copyright, licences, confidentiality, access, retention, and legal-hold rules; do not copy unnecessary source content.
4. Write independent product conclusions in `docs/RESEARCH_SYNTHESIS.md` using neutral evidence-packet IDs, not source names, links, quotations or research paths.
5. Research never becomes a decision without owner disposition in `docs/DECISION_REGISTER.md`.
6. Custody manifests may record minimal source and destination identity but must not contain narrative comparisons or product decisions.
7. Preserve immutable pre-separation archives separately and exclude them from active routing; do not silently rewrite or delete them.
