# Research Vault

Store source evidence here; it is not project commitment.

## Structure

- `sources/`: saved or linked primary material, grouped by topic/provider when useful.
- `notes/`: concise per-source findings, dates, limitations, and affected decision IDs.

## Rules

1. Prefer primary/current sources; record access date and direct link.
2. Separate sourced fact, analysis, and inference.
3. Respect copyright, licences, confidentiality, and retention rules; do not copy unnecessary source content.
4. Summarize decision-relevant findings in `docs/RESEARCH_SYNTHESIS.md` and link to evidence.
5. Research never becomes a decision without disposition in `docs/DECISION_REGISTER.md`.
