# Quality gates

## Definition gate

- Actor, problem, intended outcome and observable proof are understandable without technical translation.
- Constraints, non-goals, assumptions and human decision owner are explicit.

## Scope gate

- First demonstration is chronological and testable.
- Must/Should/Won’t and later items are distinct.
- Time box and dependencies are credible.

## Decision gate

- Credible finalists, current sources, cost/operations, risk, scalability, lock-in and exit path are explained.
- Recommendation is separate from human disposition.
- Accepted consequences have one owning destination.

## Design gate

- User and data journeys identify authoritative state, integrations and visible failure behavior.
- Security, privacy, human control and recovery are proportionate to the mode.
- Architecture and data design cover the first slice without importing later scope.

## Build-readiness gate

- First-slice brief contains inputs, outputs, acceptance tests, failure/security cases, exclusions and verification commands.
- Required decisions are accepted or explicitly defaulted; unresolved blockers are named.
- Automation router and implementation folders exist.
- Every active Markdown document is registered; local links resolve; the current handover is fresh and records repository/HEAD/deploy alignment plus authority boundaries.
- No proposed material decision or unfinished template placeholder remains in an activated build document.
- Implementation, commit and deployment authority remain separate.
