# Stakeholder interview

## Information order

Extract before asking:

1. Desired business outcome.
2. Person who experiences or operates it.
3. Current workflow or pain.
4. Visible proof that the solution works.
5. Time, cost, platform, privacy or regulatory constraints.
6. Explicit non-goals.
7. Existing systems, documents, data and decisions.

Do not require the stakeholder to answer in this structure. Accept a paragraph, transcript, screenshots, examples or existing folders; structure it for them.

## Question priority

Classify gaps:

- **Required now:** different answers materially change the current gate. Ask.
- **Defaultable:** a safe reversible assumption exists. Recommend it and label `ASSUMED`.
- **Deferred:** no current consequence. Record the trigger and do not ask.

Ask no more than three Required-now questions in one round. Prefer one when it unlocks the others.

## Decision packet

```text
D01 — [business decision]

Recommendation: [recommended option]
Why now: [decision consequence in one sentence]

A. [recommended option] — [impact]
B. [alternative] — [impact]
C. [alternative, only if credible] — [impact]

If unanswered: [safe default and assumption label, or “work pauses”]
Reply: D01 ACCEPT | D01 AMEND: ... | D01 DEFER
```

Permit `ACCEPT ALL` when every recommendation is independent and reversible. Never bundle a high-risk choice into bulk acceptance.

## Teach-back

Before advancing, restate the project as a short scenario:

> When [actor] does [action], the solution will [business result]. We know it works when [observable proof]. The first slice excludes [boundary].

If the stakeholder cannot recognize the project in this statement, repair the current gate instead of creating more documents.
