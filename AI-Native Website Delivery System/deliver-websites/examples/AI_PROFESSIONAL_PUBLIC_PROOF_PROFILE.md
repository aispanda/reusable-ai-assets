# Example profile — AI professional public-proof site

This is an illustrative project profile, not a rule for every website. Replace the working identity and validate every public claim before use.

## Accepted direction

| ID | Decision | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| D01 | New site, identity and brand | Discovery includes naming, promise, visual identity, voice, domain and provenance; it does not inherit an unrelated project brand. |
| D02 | Serve business decision-makers, technology leaders and hands-on builders | Use shared plain-language navigation; provide progressive depth, business outcomes, architecture evidence and source/code links rather than separate sites. |
| D03 | Launch with concise AI articles, engineering principles, reusable assets and a live ERD Viewer | The first release demonstrates thinking and working proof. Add résumé/skills next; defer community infrastructure until demand and operating ownership exist. |

## Public-proof journey

1. Visitor understands the AI-only planning/design/build/operate proposition.
2. Visitor reads a concise article or engineering principle.
3. Visitor discovers a reusable asset through filters, a collection or an opinionated guide.
4. Visitor opens its explanation, evidence, licence, dependencies, source repository and, where useful, a live demonstration such as the ERD Viewer.
5. Visitor contacts the owner or follows later contribution/discussion pathways.

## Staged inventory

| Stage | Include | Defer / trigger |
|---|---|---|
| Launch | Home, articles, engineering principles, reusable-asset catalogue/detail, live ERD Viewer, method/about, contact, privacy, metadata/search previews, sitemap, accessibility/performance QA | Avoid accounts, custom comments, portal and chat |
| Next | Résumé, skills, selected work/case studies, improved search/collections, optional newsletter | Add after the public-proof core is coherent |
| Community | Discussions/comments and contribution workflow | Activate only with demand, moderation owner, identity/privacy rules and contribution licensing |
| Platform | Member portal, profiles, groups/chat and richer collaborative asset creation | Separate product/security/operations decision; do not make launch architecture pay for it prematurely |

## Pattern sources to study

- Simon Willison: searchable/tagged practical publishing.
- Thoughtworks Technology Radar: dated, opinionated guidance.
- Hugging Face Hub: asset cards, collections, demos and metadata.
- Vercel Templates: actionable discovery-to-use path.
- Stripe Engineering: explain what was built, how, why and what was learned.
- GitHub Discussions: early community/contribution without immediately building a social backend.

Study the pattern and verify the current source; never clone identity, layout, prose, code or artwork.
