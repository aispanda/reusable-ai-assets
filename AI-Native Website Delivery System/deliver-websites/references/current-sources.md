## Refresh rules

- Browse official documentation before recommending a current framework version, platform limit, pricing, browser support claim, or newly changed API.
- Prefer standards and platform documentation over blog summaries.
- Treat third-party component galleries as optional sources subject to licence, accessibility, payload, and maintenance review.
- Cite current sources in the consuming project's decision record when the decision depends on them.
- Record last checked date, version where material, licence, cost/limit source, decision owner, and the trigger for re-evaluation.
- Treat showcase and inspiration sites as pattern research only; do not copy their identity, prose, visual composition, code, or media.
