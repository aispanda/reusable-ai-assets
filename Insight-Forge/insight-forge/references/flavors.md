# Writing flavors

Each flavor has one job, a reconstruction bias, and a vision weight. Match before drafting. Propose NEW FLAVOR when none fit.

## Catalog

| ID | Flavor | Job | Reconstruction bias | Vision weight | Typical length |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| F01 | Cost shock → policy | Lived burn → infrastructure/policy thesis | Economics + industrial policy | High | 8–12 min |
| F02 | Builder field note | How we tested or improved an architecture, method, or operational practice | Architecture + operations + evidence | Medium | 5–8 min |
| F03 | Policy brief | Minister/CIO one-pager | Precedents + blueprint | High | 2–4 pages |
| F04 | Opposing counsel | Attack the thesis to harden it | Failure modes + capture | Medium | 6–10 min |
| F05 | Chorus essay | Many thinkers, one join | Comparative perspectives | Medium | 8–12 min |
| F06 | White paper | Full evidence + appendix | All dimensions | High | 15–40 pages |
| F07 | Spanda longread | Contemplative × technical | Intuition + labeled imagination | High | 10–15 min |
| F08 | Incentive design | Sustainable economics for commons / OSS / utilities | Business model + metrics + flip paths | High | 8–15 min / brief |

## F08 — Incentive design (detail)

**Use when:** The core question is how money, credits, procurement, and surplus recycle into open capabilities while avoiding lock-in.

**Must include:**
- Who pays / who gets free tier
- What is rented vs owned (compute, weights, data)
- Flip conditions (vendor, model, facility)
- Surplus → builder/maintainer funding path
- Metrics private players optimize
- Failure modes (capture, metric gaming, accountability loss)

**Do not pretend:** Replacing democratic authority with “the algorithm” without human gates (see dimensions + Sutra 8 / RA-005).

## Matching script (agent → owner)

```text
Proposed flavor: F0X — <name>
Audience: <who>
Prior knowledge: <what may be assumed>
Reader outcome: <what they should explain, decide, or predict>
Depth: <brief / standard / deep>
Proof instrument (optional): <worked example / trace / calculation / state diagram / contrast / none>
Why:
- …
- …
- …
Disposition needed: ACCEPT | AMEND: … | DEFER | NEW FLAVOR: <name> — <one-job sentence>
```

If owner says the work spans two flavors, pick a **primary** for structure and a **secondary** for one section—do not mash without saying so.

Do not treat an explanatory instrument as a secondary flavor. A worked example, sequence trace, calculation, state diagram, contrast table, or metaphor is a proof choice inside the selected flavor. Use the smallest instrument that resolves the reader's actual uncertainty.
