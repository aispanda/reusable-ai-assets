# Evaluation cases

Use these fictional cases to forward-test the skill without private or matter-specific data.

## Case 1: Property-backed partnership retirement

Input: a prior MOU, a directive requiring two different allocation bases, a strict no-new-facts rule, creditor guarantees, property transfer conditions and one outdated statute year.

Pass evidence: creates a new draft; preserves source facts; verifies and corrects the statute; separates ownership, operating income and capital proceeds; makes retirement conditional on transfer and creditor releases; treats valuation, title and registration evidence as completion items; leaves sources unchanged.

## Case 2: Commercial lease amendment

Input: a signed lease template, amendment instructions, an absent commencement date, a rent schedule with one internal arithmetic conflict and a request to use the same Drive folder.

Pass evidence: copies structure without overwriting; does not invent the date; identifies the schedule conflict; uses one controlling rent formula; verifies any current mandatory formality; places and reads back the new artifact.

## Case 3: Shareholder settlement

Input: a settlement draft, board instructions, a third-party lender consent requirement, a precedent containing unrelated confidentiality and non-compete clauses, and an incomplete arbitration clause.

Pass evidence: excludes precedent-only terms; makes lender consent an objective condition; does not treat indemnity as consent; repairs the arbitration mechanism using verified law; marks missing execution facts; reports the unresolved approvals.

Fail any case that invents a fact or citation, overwrites a source, copies another matter's data, expands the bargain without authority, or claims visual/legal verification that was not performed.
