# Legal drafting gates

Run all applicable gates before final delivery.

## 1. Parties and facts

- Every name, entity, role, date, amount, percentage, dimension, asset and identifier appears in a designated source or a clearly marked blank.
- Defined terms match the operative provisions.
- No precedent-era fact survives.

## 2. Bargain and formulas

- Each economic or ownership concept has one defined basis.
- Allocation, distribution and sale formulas use the correct denominator and do not contradict one another.
- Operating income, capital proceeds, ownership and profit-sharing are separated when the sources separate them.
- An illustration cannot override the governing formula.

## 3. Conditions and timing

- Conditions precedent are objective, evidenced and assigned to a completion step.
- Core conditions cannot be satisfied by lapse of time, substantial performance or an unrelated indemnity unless the sources expressly allow it.
- Execution date, completion date and effective date are distinct when the transaction requires them.
- Waiver mechanics identify who may waive what and preserve mandatory law.

## 4. Third parties and liabilities

- The parties' agreement does not purport to bind or release a creditor, guarantor, regulator, landlord, tenant or other third party without the required consent.
- A contractual indemnity is not presented as a substitute for a third-party release.
- Pre-effective and post-effective liabilities are allocated expressly.
- Required notices, filings, registrations and publications are identified without claiming completion before evidence exists.

## 5. Property and formalities

- A memorandum or agreement does not purport to transfer title when a separate stamped, registered or otherwise formal instrument is required.
- Property descriptions are sufficient for the requested document stage; missing title identifiers remain explicit completion items.
- Encumbrance, consent, valuation and title evidence are conditions where the sources require them.
- Appurtenant rights and residual property treatment are consistent with the principal allocation.

## 6. Current law and dispute clause

- Statute title, year, current status, commencement, repeal and savings are verified.
- Case status and appellate history are verified when case law is relied upon.
- Arbitration or forum provisions specify a workable appointment mechanism, seat or jurisdiction, language when requested, and interim-relief treatment.
- Tax language requires professional determination and does not invent a tax characterization.

## 7. Scope and prose

- No optional deal term was added without authority.
- The strongest implementation rule appears before illustrations and exceptions.
- Clauses use concrete triggers and consequences rather than rhetorical assurances.
- Repetition, hidden inconsistency, AI meta-commentary and unsupported certainty have been removed.

## 8. Artifact and handoff

- The final document is a new artifact unless in-place editing was authorized.
- Source files are unchanged and final placement is verified.
- Required document rendering, connector readback, citation checking and page inspection passed or the limitation is disclosed.
- The handoff lists only material corrections and real unresolved items.
